How to Use This Tool

This generator creates clean, high-resolution comment cards without taking messy screenshots from your phone.

  1. Select the Layout: Choose a generic style that matches where the comment came from (e.g., Video App Style for YouTube).
  2. Input the Data: Type the username, exact text, and likes. Use the @ symbol to automatically highlight usernames in blue.
  3. Upload Avatars: Upload the commenter's profile picture. If applicable to the layout, upload your own logo for the "Creator Liked" badge.
  4. Toggle Features: Add verified badges, timestamps, or "edited" marks to make it look authentic.
  5. Download: Click the export button to instantly download a transparent, high-res PNG file. No watermarks!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this comment generator free to use? ›
Yes, our Universal Comment Card Generator is 100% free. There are no limits on how many graphics you can create.
Are there any watermarks on the final image? ›
No. We believe creator tools should be clean. The downloaded PNG will be completely watermark-free.
Why do the layout options have generic names? ›
To respect corporate trademarks and community guidelines, we use descriptive layout names (like "Video App Style") rather than official brand names. The visual output remains highly accurate to your favorite platforms.
Does this tool upload my images to a server? ›
No. All image processing and rendering happens entirely within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your uploaded avatars and texts remain completely private and are never sent to our servers.
How do I highlight a username in blue? ›
Simply type the @ symbol followed by the username in the text box (e.g., @socialmediatoolshub). The tool will automatically detect it and color it blue to mimic native platform behavior.
What is the Creator Liked Badge? ›
On certain photo-sharing apps, when the author of a post likes a comment, a tiny profile picture with a red heart appears next to the timestamp. Our tool lets you toggle this on for maximum realism.
Can I use these images for commercial videos? ›
Yes. You can use the generated graphics in your YouTube videos, Shorts, TikToks, and Instagram Reels.
