Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe and private? ›
Absolutely. Bulk Image Compressor runs 100% locally in your web browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server, keeping your content entirely private.
What image formats are supported? ›
We support JPEG, PNG, and WebP for both upload and conversion. You can easily switch formats while optimizing your files.
Is there a file limit for processing? ›
There is no hard limit, but because processing happens in your browser's memory (RAM), we recommend batches of up to 50 images for the best performance.
Does this tool work on mobile devices? ›
Yes! This tool is fully responsive and works on any modern smartphone browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge). Tap 'Select Files' to browse your photo gallery.
Can I convert PNG images to WebP? ›
Yes. Upload your PNG files and select 'WEBP' from the format dropdown menu in the file queue before downloading.
Will compression reduce my image quality? ›
You have full control. Setting quality to 80% usually reduces file size by 70% with no visible loss in quality. 100% quality performs lossless compression where possible.
Can I download all optimized images at once? ›
Yes, once all files in your queue are processed, simply click the 'Download All (ZIP)' button to save them all in one archive.
Is this image tool really free to use? ›
Yes, everything at SocialMediaToolsHub is 100% free for creators. No subscriptions, no credits, just fast utilities.
