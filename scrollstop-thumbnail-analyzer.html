About ScrollStop Technology

ScrollStop is built on the psychological principles of pre-attentive processing. In high-velocity social feeds, users spend approximately 100ms per item before deciding whether to stop or continue. This tool simulates that initial window.

Our heuristic engine performs real-time pixel-by-pixel analysis using your browser's local GPU. We measure luminance variance, edge density, and frequency distribution to determine how "eye-catching" an image truly is.

The Analysis Pillars

  • Luminance Contrast Variance
  • High-Frequency Edge Noise
  • Asymmetric Balance Distribution
  • Lizard-Brain Color Triggering
