Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 03, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to SocialMediaToolsHub ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website at https://socialmediatoolshub.com.

Our core philosophy is simple: we build tools that run in your browser. This means, in the vast majority of cases, your data (such as images you edit or text you generate) never leaves your device.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to ensure the website functions correctly and to analyze how our tools are used.

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous information about how you access and use the Service, such as your browser type, operating system, the pages you visit, the time spent on those pages, and other diagnostic data.
  • Cookies and Local Storage: We use local storage to save your preferences (like Dark Mode or tool settings). This data remains on your device.

Important Note on User Content: When you use our tools to process files (such as the Image Splitter, Watermark Tool, or SVG ASMR Recorder), the processing happens 100% client-side within your web browser. Your images and videos are not uploaded to our servers and we do not have access to them.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To monitor the usage of our Service (via Google Analytics) to understand which tools are popular and need improvement.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues.
  • To respond to your inquiries if you contact us.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our Service and hold certain information.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function (e.g., remembering your session).
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by third-party services like Google Analytics to report anonymous usage statistics.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, some portions of our Service may not function properly.

5. Third-Party Services

We may employ third-party companies and services to facilitate our Service. These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to monitor and analyze the use of our Service. Google Analytics collects data such as your IP address and device type. You can opt-out of having made your activity on the Service available to Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Formspree: If you use our contact form, your message details (Name, Email, Message) are processed by Formspree to deliver the email to us. Formspree does not use this data for their own marketing.

6. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. Because our tools process your files locally on your device, your creative content is inherently secure as it is never transmitted over the internet to our servers.

For the anonymous analytics data we do collect, we use commercially acceptable means to protect it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your Children has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

8. Links to Other Websites

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
