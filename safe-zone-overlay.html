How it Works & Why You Need It

Every platform draws buttons, captions and icons on top of your video. This tool simulates those overlays so you can design around them and keep your subtitles readable.

🚫 Problem: UI hides your subtitles
✅ Solution: Use safe zone guides

Without a safe zone, your captions and calls-to-action often sit underneath like buttons, profile photos, or the comment bar. By previewing your frame with this social media safe zone overlay, you can move text higher, keep faces clear, and avoid unprofessional-looking videos where important words are cut off.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how social media safe zones work on TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts, and how this overlay tool keeps your content inside those areas.

What is a social media safe zone? ›
A social media safe zone is the area of your vertical video that stays visible after each platform adds its own interface elements. Apps like TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts place like buttons, captions, profile photos, progress bars and comments on top of your content, which means the edges and bottom of the frame are often partially hidden. Designing inside the safe zone ensures that your titles, subtitles and calls-to-action remain readable on most devices.
How does this safe zone overlay tool work? ›
This tool lets you drag and drop a screenshot or frame from your vertical video. It then superimposes a transparent guide that approximates where UI overlays appear for the selected platform. The coloured “danger zones” mark regions where buttons, captions or navigation bars commonly sit, and the highlighted safe area shows where text and important subjects are less likely to be covered.
Is my image data private and secure? ›
Yes. This safe zone checker runs entirely inside your browser. Images are never uploaded to any server — they stay on your device. That makes it safe to test client work, drafts, and unposted videos without worrying about privacy or leaks.
Which platforms are supported? ›
The overlay currently supports:
  • TikTok full-screen vertical videos
  • Instagram Reels safe zones
  • Facebook Reels interface layout
  • YouTube Shorts vertical videos
Each option uses a slightly different arrangement of danger and safe areas based on where the platform UI usually sits, so you can quickly switch between overlays and adjust one master edit for multiple uploads.
Why are my subtitles getting cut off on Reels or TikTok? ›
Subtitles often get cut off because they’re placed too low in the frame or too close to the right edge. Platform UI, device aspect ratios and even small design updates can shift where captions, buttons and progress bars appear. On TikTok and Instagram Reels for example, the bottom area is frequently covered by the main caption and audio line, while the right side is stacked with like, comment and share icons. Using a social media safe zone overlay when you design your video helps you keep subtitles and key text away from these shifting UI areas.
