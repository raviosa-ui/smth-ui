How to Land Deals with this Media Kit

Brands receive hundreds of text emails a day. Attaching a clean, data-driven visual graphic makes you stand out instantly.

  1. Set the Vibe: Pick an accent color that matches your brand or the brand you are pitching.
  2. Let us do the Math: Enter your followers and average views. We will calculate your true engagement rate—the #1 metric brands look for.
  3. Define the Package: Don't leave them guessing. List exactly what you will deliver (e.g., "1 YouTube Video + 1 Insta Story").
  4. Social Proof: Upload logos of software or products you've worked with to build instant trust.
  5. Export & Attach: Download the PNG and attach it directly to your pitch email. If pitching in person, they can scan your custom QR code!

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the engagement rate calculator work? ›
Simply enter your total followers/subscribers and your average views or likes per post. The tool automatically divides average views by total followers and multiplies by 100 to give brands an accurate engagement percentage.
What is the QR Code used for? ›
If you are pitching in person or sending a PDF, a brand manager can scan the QR code on your pitch card to instantly open your YouTube channel, Instagram profile, or a specific viral video link.
Why should I include 'Trusted By' logos? ›
Adding logos of brands you have previously collaborated with provides instant social proof. It tells new brands that you are professional, reliable, and trusted by the industry.
What are Smart Labels? ›
When you select your primary platform (e.g., YouTube vs. Instagram), the tool automatically updates the metric labels to match industry standards, such as swapping 'Followers' to 'Subscribers'.
Is my data uploaded to your servers? ›
No. The entire rendering process, including image uploads and QR code generation, happens locally in your web browser. Your data is 100% private.
How should I send this to brands? ›
You can attach the downloaded high-resolution PNG directly to your outreach emails, embed it in a PDF media kit, or send it in DMs to brand managers on LinkedIn or Instagram.
