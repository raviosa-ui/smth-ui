Every Question Creators Actually Ask

Why does TikTok always zoom in and ruin my video?
Because you exported 16:9 (YouTube ratio) instead of 9:16. TikTok forces full-screen vertical → it auto-zooms and cuts heads/feet. Always export 1080×1920!
I recorded vertically on my phone — why are there black bars on TikTok?
Your phone has a taller screen than 9:16 (usually 19.5:9 or 20:9). Export at 1080×1920 or 1080×2400 → no more black bars.
1080×1920 vs 1080×2340 — which one should I use in 2025?
1080×1920 = 100% safe everywhere. 1080×2340–2400 = fills newer phones perfectly (iPhone 15 Pro, Galaxy S24) but adds tiny black bars on older devices. Most creators stick to 1080×1920.
Instagram cropped my beautiful portrait photo — help!
Instagram Feed only loves 4:5 (1080×1350), 1:1 (1080×1080) or 16:9. Anything else gets cropped. Use 1080×1350 for tall portraits → no cropping!
Can I post a horizontal YouTube video on Reels without it looking terrible?
Only if you add blurred side panels (use our “Full Frame Border Blur” tool) or crop to 9:16. Otherwise Reels will add huge black bars or zoom in automatically.
What resolution do the top creators actually export?
99% use 1080×1920. It’s the sweet spot: maximum quality + works perfectly on TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Stories, and even Instagram Feed when cropped.
My iPhone screenshot shows 1290×2796 — is that good?
That’s iPhone 15 Pro Max with Dynamic Island. For perfect TikTok use 1179×2555 or just export 1080×1920 (everyone does the latter — zero issues).
Where should I put text so it never gets cut off?
Keep everything inside the center “safe zone” (about 80% of the screen). Check with our Safe Zone Overlay tool — it shows exactly where UI elements hide your content.
Do I lose quality if I export at 720p instead of 1080p?
Yes — TikTok/Reels compress everything anyway, but 1080p source = noticeably sharper final video. Always export 1080 width minimum.
