How to create an Instagram Grid Banner

This tool takes your large photo and slices it into perfect squares.

  1. Upload a high-quality image (landscape orientation usually works best).
  2. Choose how many rows you want (1, 2, or 3). We always split into 3 columns.
  3. The tool will automatically center-crop your image to fit the squares.
  4. Click download. Important: Your browser will download the images one by one. You may need to "allow multiple downloads".
  5. Post them to Instagram in reverse order (bottom-right first, top-left last).
