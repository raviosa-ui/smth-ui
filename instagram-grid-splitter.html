How to create an Instagram Grid Banner

  1. Upload a high-quality image (landscape orientation usually works best for banners).
  2. Choose how many rows you want (1, 2, or 3). We always split into 3 columns to match Instagram's layout.
  3. The tool will automatically center-crop your image to fit the square tiles.
  4. Click Download in Zip for a single file, or Split & Download All for individual files.
  5. Post them to Instagram in reverse order (start with the last image numbered 1, end with the first image).
