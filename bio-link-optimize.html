Frequently Asked Questions

▼
How do short URLs work?
We create a memorable, shortened version of your bio link that redirects to your full bio page. Perfect for social media bios with character limits.
Can I track clicks on my links?
Yes! Our built-in analytics track total clicks and individual link performance. Data is stored locally in your browser.
What are QR codes used for?
QR codes make your bio link scannable from physical materials like business cards, flyers, or presentations.
Can I use my own custom slug?
Yes, choose a custom ending for your short URL (e.g., socialmediatoolshub.com/bio/yourname).
Is my data private?
All data stays in your browser. We don't store your links or analytics on our servers.
How long do my bio links last?
Your bio links work as long as you have them saved in your browser. Clear your browser data to remove them.
