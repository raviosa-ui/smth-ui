Frequently Asked Questions — Hashtag Cleaner

        


          
          

            
            

              

                The Hashtag Cleaner on SocialMediaToolsHub extracts and normalizes hashtags from mixed caption text. It strips punctuation and stray characters, removes duplicates, and returns a tidy block you can paste into Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook. Use it when copying captions from notes, chats, or other apps to ensure hashtags remain valid and compact.

                Deduplication is case-insensitive: the tool treats #Travel and #travel as the same tag and keeps the first instance. Output is normalized to lowercase to avoid accidental duplicates and ensure consistent behavior across platforms. You can edit capitalization manually after cleaning if needed.
