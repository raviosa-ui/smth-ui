Frequently Asked Questions — Hashtag Cleaner

        

          

            
What does the Hashtag Cleaner do and when should I use it?

            

              The Hashtag Cleaner on SocialMediaToolsHub is a lightweight browser utility that extracts and normalizes hashtags from a block of text or a mixed caption. When creators copy captions from notes, messaging apps, or other tools they often bring along punctuation, stray characters, emojis, or duplicate tags. This tool isolates hashtag-like tokens, strips trailing punctuation and non-hashtag characters, and returns a compact, deduplicated list that you can paste directly into Instagram, TikTok or Facebook. Use it when you want reliable, clean hashtag blocks fast — especially helpful before posting from a phone or scheduling tool.
            


            
Is my text uploaded anywhere or saved on a server?

            

              No. Hashtag Cleaner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to SocialMediaToolsHub’s servers by default — the tool performs all extraction, deduplication and formatting locally. This keeps your draft captions private and ensures the cleaning step is immediate, even when you are offline or on a slow connection. If you choose to copy or download the output locally, those actions happen on your device only.
            


            
How does deduplication work and will it change capitalization?

            

              Deduplication is case-insensitive: the tool treats #Travel and #travel as the same tag and will keep only the first occurrence in the cleaned list. Output is normalized to lowercase to reduce accidental duplicates and to keep blocks compact and consistent. If you prefer specific capitalization for branding, you can edit the final output manually before copying. The goal is functional hashtags that behave consistently across platforms.
            


            
Does the cleaner support Unicode or non-English hashtags?

            

              Yes — while the cleaning uses broadly-compatible patterns to maximize browser support, common Unicode letters and digits are preserved when possible and the routine removes punctuation and stray symbols that break tags. For edge-case scripts or platform-specific rules, test a sample tag; the cleaner is conservative and designed to produce widely compatible hashtag tokens.
            


            
What about blacklist and automatic hashify features?

            

              SocialMediaToolsHub provides options (if enabled) to automatically convert plain words into hashtag tokens (hashify) and to apply a local blacklist of tags you want to exclude permanently, such as known spammy or shadowed tags. Blacklist settings are stored locally so they do not leave your device. If you haven’t enabled blacklist or hashify, the cleaner simply extracts existing hashtags and returns a deduplicated list.
            


            
How can I prepare tag groups for platforms that limit hashtags?

            

              The Hashtag Cleaner supports sorting and formatting so you can craft groups manually: sort by length or alphabetically, then copy the first 30 tags for an Instagram post. Planned updates on SocialMediaToolsHub will add a one-click 30-per-group feature with separate copy buttons, but for now sorting and manual grouping are reliable and immediate.
            


            
What should I do if a tag still doesn’t behave on the platform?

            

              Occasionally a platform may block or ignore specific tags. If a tag appears broken after pasting (for example it doesn’t link or search), remove any non-alphanumeric characters and retry. If a tag is known to be shadowed, add it to your local blacklist in the tool and enable blacklist mode to prevent it from being included in future outputs.
            


            
Which browsers and devices are supported?

            

              The Hashtag Cleaner works in modern browsers that support standard JavaScript and standard regular expressions. That includes current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile. If you notice a browser-specific issue, include your browser name and version when reporting it so the SocialMediaToolsHub team can investigate.
            


            
Where can I get help or report bugs?

            

              If something doesn’t work or you have a suggestion, contact the SocialMediaToolsHub support/contact page with a short description and a sample input that reproduces the problem. Provide your browser and device details — those help reproduce and fix issues quickly. The project is focused on fast local tools, so feedback is valuable and usually addressed in small, frequent updates.
