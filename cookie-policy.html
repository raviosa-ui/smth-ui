Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on SocialMediaToolsHub

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are and how SocialMediaToolsHub uses them. We keep things simple: we only use cookies to understand how visitors use our free tools so we can keep improving them.

Last updated: December 3, 2025
What Are Cookies? ›
Cookies are tiny text files that a website places on your computer or mobile device when you visit it. They help the site remember certain information (like language preferences) and allow website owners to see how people use their site.
How We Use Cookies ›
SocialMediaToolsHub is a completely free utility hub. We do not show ads, sell data, or track you across the web.

The only cookies we use are performance/analytical cookies from Google Analytics 4 (GA4). These help us see things like:
  • How many people visit the site
  • Which tools are most popular
  • How visitors move between pages
This information lets us fix bugs faster and build the tools you actually want.
Types of Cookies Used ›
We only use the following:

Performance/Analytical Cookies (Third-party)
Set by Google Analytics 4 (GA4). These are placed by Google (a third party), not by us directly.

No Strictly Necessary Cookies
Our site works perfectly without any cookies at all — the GA4 cookies are optional and only used for site improvement.

We do not use advertising cookies, targeting cookies, or social media cookies.
Managing Your Cookie Preferences ›
When you first visit SocialMediaToolsHub, a small cookie banner appears asking for your consent.

• You can accept the analytics cookies
• You can decline them — the site works exactly the same

You can also delete or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers let you:
  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Clear all cookies when you close the browser
If you disable cookies, all our tools will still work perfectly.
Contact Us ›
If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or how we handle data, please visit our Contact Us page.

We don’t have a formal Data Protection Officer because we collect the absolute minimum amount of data possible — but we’re always happy to help.
