Why Preview Your Thumbnail?

Your thumbnail never exists in a vacuum. It competes with dozens of other images, colors, and texts on the screen. A design that looks great in Photoshop might vanish against YouTube's dark background or become unreadable when shrunk down to mobile size.

The "Context Gap": Most creators fail because they don't check legibility. By using the 4 modes above, you can ensure your text is large enough for mobile users (70% of traffic) and that your colors pop against the "Up Next" sidebar.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the correct YouTube thumbnail size?
The ideal size is 1280x720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio). Minimum width is 640 pixels.
Why use Emojis in the preview?
We use emojis and colors to simulate "noisy" competitor thumbnails without using heavy images. This mimics the distraction of the YouTube feed.
Is my image uploaded?
No. All processing happens in your browser. Your image never leaves your device.
