Frequently asked questions — Caption Counter

What are Instagram caption limits and best practices?
1
Instagram allows up to 2,200 characters in a caption. However, the first ~125 characters are crucial because Instagram shows a "…more" link after that. For better engagement, put your hook or CTA within the first 100–125 characters, use line breaks to improve readability, and keep essential hashtags near the top or in the first comment (if you prefer a cleaner caption).
What's the Instagram bio character limit and tips for bios?
2
Instagram bios are limited to 150 characters. Use SocialMediaToolsHub's bio preview to check how your handle, line breaks and emojis render. Prioritise key info (who you are + value) and include one link or a link service if you need multiple destinations.
How does Twitter/X character counting work?
3
Twitter/X posts are limited to 280 characters. Mentions, hashtags and emojis count as characters. Use the preview to check truncation and reduce long URLs or move extra details to a thread. The counter also shows remaining characters in real time.
Why are emojis counted separately and how do they affect length?
4
Emojis are counted as characters and often count as one visual token but multiple Unicode codepoints. Our emoji counter helps you track heavy emoji usage so your caption stays within platform limits and maintains readability.
What are the YouTube options: Title (Shorts) vs Description?
5
SocialMediaToolsHub provides two YouTube presets: YouTube Title (Shorts) — optimized to 100 characters for the short upload title, and YouTube Description — full description up to 5,000 characters. Use the title for a sharp hook and the description for more context, links, and timestamps.
What are Top Keywords and how do they help with captions?
6
The Top Keywords panel extracts meaningful words from your caption and excludes common stopwords. This helps you spot themes to convert into hashtags, improve discoverability, and make captions keyword-rich without stuffing. The tool shows keyword frequency to prioritise the best tags.
How does the first-line hook analysis improve engagement?
7
The first-line hook analysis scores the first 125 characters for elements that stop the scroll: questions, emojis, power words and CTAs. A strong hook increases clicks, saves and comment rates. Use the suggested chips to add a question or short CTA to boost engagement.
What does Auto truncate do and when should I use it?
8
Auto truncate prevents writing beyond the selected platform limit by trimming text as you type or paste. Use it when composing platform-specific captions (e.g., Twitter/X or YouTube Title) to ensure your final copy won't be cut off when posting.
How do I clean and fix formatting with Fix Caption?
9
Fix Caption removes invisible characters, normalises line breaks, collapses extra blank lines and trims stray spaces before punctuation. It preserves your words but ensures captions post cleanly across Instagram, Twitter/X and YouTube.
How many captions can I save and can I export them?
10
Your browser stores up to 30 saved captions locally. Use the Export feature to download them as a text file for content calendars, scheduling tools, or team handoffs. Saved captions remain in local storage unless you clear history.
Does SocialMediaToolsHub collect my captions or data?
11
No. All processing — counting, previews, keyword extraction and hook analysis — is performed locally in your browser. SocialMediaToolsHub does not send captions to external servers, and saved entries remain in local storage on your device.
Best practices for hashtags, mentions and URLs in captions?
12
Keep hashtags relevant and concise — Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post but quality beats quantity. Mentions count as characters, so use handles sparingly in the first line. URLs count fully toward character limits and are not clickable in captions; put important links in your profile bio or use link services and add them to the first comment if needed.
