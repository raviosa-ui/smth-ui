Frequently asked questions — Caption Counter

What are Instagram caption limits and best practices?
1
Instagram allows up to 2,200 characters. The first ~125 characters are crucial because of the "…more" link. Put hooks/CTAs early and use line breaks for readability.
What's the Instagram bio character limit and tips for bios?
2
Instagram bios are 150 characters. Use the bio preview to verify handle, emojis and spacing. Prioritise your key info and a single link.
How does Twitter/X character counting work?
3
Twitter/X posts are 280 characters. Mentions, hashtags and emojis count; use the preview to check truncation and move extra info to a thread.
Why are emojis counted separately and how do they affect length?
4
Emojis count as characters and can use multiple codepoints. The emoji counter helps manage heavy emoji use to avoid exceeding limits.
What are the YouTube options: Title (Shorts) vs Description?
5
Two presets: YouTube Title (Shorts) — 100 chars for the short title, and YouTube Description — 5,000 chars for full descriptions. Use the title for hooks and the description for links and timestamps.
How does the first-line hook analysis improve engagement?
6
The tool checks the first 125 chars for questions, emojis, power words and CTAs and rates hook strength. Adding a question or short CTA often boosts engagement.
What does Auto truncate do and when should I use it?
7
Auto truncate trims text to the selected platform limit as you type. Use it when you want platform-safe captions (e.g., Twitter or YouTube Title).
How do I clean and fix formatting with Fix Caption?
8
Fix Caption removes invisible characters, normalises line breaks, collapses extra blank lines and trims spaces before punctuation for cleaner posts.
How many captions can I save and can I export them?
9
You can save up to 30 captions locally. Export downloads saved captions as a text file for planning and scheduling.
Can I use this tool for other platforms like Facebook or LinkedIn?
10
Yes — choose the custom preset and set the desired character limit. The counter and formatting tools are generic and work for any platform.
Does SocialMediaToolsHub collect my captions or data?
11
No. All processing runs locally in your browser. Saved captions remain in local storage and are not uploaded.
Best practices for hashtags, mentions and URLs in captions?
12
Keep hashtags relevant (Instagram allows up to 30). Mentions count as characters. URLs are not clickable in captions — use your bio or link services for important links.
