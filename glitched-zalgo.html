Frequently Asked Questions

How does this work?

This tool adds Unicode combining characters to your letters. Combining marks attach to the preceding character and visually distort it — creating the glitched or 'Zalgo' look.

Will it work on Instagram / Twitter / TikTok?

Most platforms will display the characters, but extremely heavy combinations can be blocked or may look broken. Use moderate intensity for captions and bios.

Is any data sent to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored externally.
