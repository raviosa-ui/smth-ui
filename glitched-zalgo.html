Frequently Asked Questions — SocialMediaToolsHub

What is glitched or Zalgo text?

Glitched (often called Zalgo) text combines normal letters with many Unicode combining marks (diacritics) to produce a corrupted, twisted appearance. The marks attach to characters and visually 'break' them for a spooky or hacker-like effect.

How is 'glitched' different from 'Zalgo'?

'Zalgo' usually means extreme, horror-style distortion (lots of marks), while 'glitched' is a broader term that includes subtle, stylized, or cyber/hacker looks used for aesthetic effect.

Why is this trend popular?

It fits visual-first platforms and subcultures (Gen Z, vaporwave, cyberpunk, horror). The odd appearance draws attention in busy feeds and pairs well with short-form video, memes and themed campaigns.
